Delete your TapSense account

TapSense is a personal-finance app with an optional smart-wallet accessory. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1 — In App (Instant)

  1. Open TapSense and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2 — Via Email (if you no longer have the app)

Email tapsense.app.sg@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my TapSense account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your login (email and password), profile and name, transactions, categories and budgets, goals, paired wallets, wallet events and wallet notifications. Voice notes and drafts are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall it.

What we keep

Nothing is kept in your account after deletion. Server backups made by our hosting provider expire automatically within 7 days. Anonymous crash reports (no name, email or transaction data) may be kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
