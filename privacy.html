TapSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

TapSense (“the app”, “we”) is a personal-finance app that helps you log and understand your spending, with an optional TapSense smart-wallet accessory. It is made by TapSense Team, as a part of the Innovation By Design Course at SUTD, for the completion of Masters in Technology & Design in Singapore. Our team members are Simran Srinivasan Chikkala, Jerrin Shaji George, Guo Sheng, Wei Xin, Liu Chao. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. We handle personal data in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

2. Phone permissions

  • Bluetooth (and, on older Android versions, location, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning). Used only to find and talk to your TapSense wallet. We do not record your location.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you are recording a voice log.
  • Camera: only while you are scanning a QR code.
  • Notifications: to show alerts.
  • Usage access (Android, optional): if you turn on QR payment detection, the app checks on your phone which payment app was open in the last minute, to suggest a transaction draft. This list of apps never leaves your phone.
  • Installed payment apps: to offer the “Pay with…” chooser, the app checks on your phone which banking and payment apps are installed. This is not uploaded.

3. AI processing (only if you turn it on)

TapSense is powered by Google Gemini. AI is off until you choose to turn it on, either when the app first asks or later in Settings → AI, and you can turn it off at any time.

When AI is on, the app sends your voice notes (the audio or the spoken text), together with the list of category names, to Google’s Gemini API through our server, to fill in the amount, merchant and category, and uses Gemini to write your weekly spending summary from your category totals. Your name and email are never included.

We use Gemini’s free tier. Under Google’s terms for the free tier, Google may use this content to provide, improve and develop its products, and human reviewers at Google may read it (after Google separates it from our account). Please do not say card numbers or other sensitive details in voice notes.

When AI is off, nothing is sent to Gemini. Voice notes are still saved so you can fill in the details yourself, and weekly insights are calculated on your phone instead.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your data and we do not use it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers that run the app, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions (servers in Southeast Asia - Singapore).
  • Google Firebase: push notifications and crash reports.
  • Google Gemini API: AI processing described in section 3.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

5. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Each user can read and change only their own data (enforced by database row-level security). AI keys are held on our server, not in the app.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database immediately. Backups made by our hosting provider expire within BACKUP_DAYS days, and crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account, or follow these steps if you no longer have the app.
  • Delete transactions: individually, or all at once in Settings.
  • Access or correct your data: most of it can be viewed and edited in the app; for anything else, email us.
  • Turn off notifications, QR payment detection or Bluetooth at any time in the app or your phone’s settings.

8. Children

TapSense is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
